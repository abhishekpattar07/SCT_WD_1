About the fest

A commerce college
that ships software

TechSpark started in 2024 as one coding day inside the BCA department. Version 2.0 opens the whole campus to every college in the region.

A.S. Patil College of Commerce has taught in Vijayapura since 1961. It is an autonomous, NAAC-accredited institution affiliated to Rani Channamma University, Belagavi, running B.Com, BBA, BCA, M.Com and MBA programmes.

For two days in October the campus stops being a lecture hall. Teams take a problem at ten in the morning and demo a working answer the next afternoon. Every event is judged by faculty alongside invited industry mentors, against rubrics published before it starts.

“We wanted an event where a B.Com student and an engineering student sit at the same table and argue about the same bug.”

Organising Committee, Department of Computer Applications
  • Register once, enter up to three events
  • Lunch and refreshments on both days
  • Certificates emailed within seven days
  • Free pre-fest workshops on Git, APIs and design

Events

Six flagship competitions

Click any card to open the rules, team size and what to bring.

01

Hackathon

Build a working prototype in twenty-four hours from a problem revealed on stage.

Teams of 2–424 hours
  • Themes: campus problems, fintech for small traders, accessibility
  • Bring your own laptop and charger, Wi-Fi and power supplied
  • Any stack allowed, but code must be written during the event
  • Eight-minute demo and questions before the panel
02

CodeSprint

Eight problems, one ranked leaderboard. Speed and correctness both count.

Solo entry3 hours
  • Languages: C, C++, Java, Python
  • Problems graded beginner to advanced, first-years welcome
  • Ties broken by total submission time
  • Lab machines provided, no personal devices inside
03

UI/UX Challenge

Redesign a real service and defend every decision you made to the panel.

Teams of 24 hours
  • Briefs: college fee portal, city bus pass, shop billing screen
  • Figma, Adobe XD, Canva or paper wireframes all accepted
  • Deliver three screens and a one-page reasoning note
  • Judged on usability and clarity, not decoration
04

Tech Quiz

Computing history, current news, logic puzzles, then a rapid buzzer finish.

Teams of 22 rounds + final
  • Written prelims on Day 1, top six teams reach the stage final
  • Rounds: origins, current affairs, guess the logo, buzzer
  • Negative marking applies only in the buzzer round
  • The quizmaster's decision is final
05

Project Expo

Bring something finished and demo it from your own stall all day.

Teams of 1–4Day 2
  • Table, power point and display board provided per team
  • Submit a 200-word abstract by 9 October 2026
  • Judged on originality, a working demo and real-world use
  • Separate visitors' choice award decided by open voting
06

Robo Challenge

Line-following track, then an obstacle arena. Fastest clean run wins.

Teams of 2–4Day 2
  • Bot limit 25 × 25 × 25 cm, battery powered only
  • Wired or wireless control both allowed
  • Two attempts per team, the better run counts
  • Repair pit with basic tools available on site

Nine smaller events run alongside these — typing race, debugging duel, poster design, spreadsheet showdown and more. The full list goes to registered participants.
